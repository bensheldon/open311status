Extreme Weather Notification In case of a Heat Warning or a Wind Chill Warning, the City will activate the Extreme Weather Notification System to provide weather information to those with special needs. Through its Emergency Telephone Notification System (or 911 Callback) the City will contact registered households with a recorded telephone message that will provide (1) weather forecasts and safety tips, (2) City services information (e.g. cooling/warming centers), and (3) how to register for an in-person well-being check.
Park Repair/Maintenance This type code should be used to report facilities management issues , and all issues dealing with needed maintenance work at Chicago Park District Facilities and Grounds. In the description, please note the name of the park or park district facility.
Commercial Fire Safety Inspection - FAC This service request is used to ask the Fire Department to schedule an inspection to determine whether fire hazards exist in a commercial property. If a residential inspection is needed enter a building violation instead.
Bungalow/Vintage Home Information Request This service request is used to request the mailing of information on technical assistance to current and would-be owners of Historic Chicago Bungalows and Vintage Homes. Information will be sent to you that can guide you in the purchase, restoration, or modernization of these homes, and help you find the resources you need.
Home Buyer Program Info Request Department of Housing offers different programs to assist homebuyers looking to purchase their primary residence in the City of Chicago. Programs are limited to funding availability and applicants must meet ALL eligibility requirements. Please provide name and email address or mailing address to receive additional information.
Small Accessible Repairs and Ramps for Seniors Provides enabling devices to 1-to-4 unit residences occupied by low and moderate income seniors who are at least 60 years of age. The program addresses issues of accessibility, safety, and security by providing needed upgrades, including grab bars, ramps, and other needed repairs so seniors can maintain their independence.
